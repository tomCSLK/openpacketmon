openpacketmon shows the network traffic of this computer in its own window.
Based on Sniffnet.
Features:
Choose a network adapter, a capture file (PCAP/PCAPNG) or an IPFIX collector as the source;
Filter the observed traffic with BPF expressions and write it to a PCAP file;
View overall statistics and a live chart of the traffic (bytes, bits or packets per second);
Get domain names, network providers (AS) and countries of the remote hosts;
See which programs exchange traffic;
Search, sort and inspect every network connection;
List single packets with their layers and bytes, with a display filter, and save them as PCAP;
Follow streams and see TCP problems and response times in the statistics;
Mark hosts, programs and IP addresses as favorites and get notified about their traffic;
Light and dark design, English and German.
